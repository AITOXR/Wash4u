Our Promise

Why We Choose to Clean Differently

Wash4You exists to give busy families and professionals in Gurugram their time back. We believe laundry shouldn't be a weekend chore — it should be a seamless service that just works. Every garment we clean is treated with care, every delivery is on time, and every product we use is chosen with your health and the environment in mind.

Our Vanzoo cleaning system uses European/Italian hydrocarbon dry cleaning technology — a low-temperature, solvent-based process that is gentle on fabrics, safe for sensitive skin, and significantly kinder to the environment than traditional perchloroethylene (perc) methods. It preserves colour, shape, and texture while delivering a deep, effective clean.

  • Biodegradable, non-toxic detergents across all services
  • Italian hydrocarbon dry cleaning — gentler than traditional PERC
  • Low-temperature processes that preserve fabric integrity
  • Water-efficient cleaning that reduces environmental impact
