Privacy & Security Policy

Information We Collect

We collect customer information including: name, address, and contact details; records of services used and payments made; preferences and customer feedback.

How Information is Used

Customer data is used to manage logistics including pickups, cleaning, and delivery; send service updates, confirmations, and invoices; enhance service quality; and share promotional offers (not if customer opts out).

Data Protection

All information is stored on secure, encrypted servers. Payments are processed through PCI-DSS-compliant gateways. We do not retain credit or debit card information. All employees follow strict confidentiality policies.

Data Sharing

Information is shared only with logistics and payment partners under secure agreements. Wash4You does not sell or share data for external marketing purposes.

Cookies & Analytics

Our website may use cookies for analytics and user functionality. Cookies do not store sensitive information and can be disabled in browser settings.

Customer Rights

Customers may access, update, or request deletion of their personal data by contacting care@wash4you.in.

Data Retention

Personal data is retained only as long as needed for service and regulatory compliance. Anonymized data may be used for reporting or auditing.
