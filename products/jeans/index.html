    Fabric Preservation

    Why Professional Jeans Care?

    Denim is durable, but regular home washing machines and harsh agitators fade indigo dyes, twist outseams, and degrade elastane fibers over time. Here is why professional care makes the difference:

    01

    Colour & Indigo Retention

    Home detergents and harsh agitation wash out rich indigo dye. Our low-friction process preserves color depth and prevents uneven streakiness.

    02

    Shape & Seam Integrity

    Spin cycles twist denim leg seams and stretch out waistbands. Professional finishing resets the original leg contour and silhouette.

    03

    Stretch & Elastane Protection

    Modern stretch denim contains elastane that degrades under high dryer heat. We use calibrated temperatures to protect stretch recovery.

    04

    Hardware & Stitching Longevity

    Rivets, brass zippers, and heavy chain-stitching stay intact without snagging or rusting during our gentle cleaning cycles.

    05

    Surface Finish & Soft Texture

    Say goodbye to the stiff, cardboard-like feeling of air-dried denim. High-pressure steam softens the weave while crisping edges.

    06

    Wrinkle Management

    Stubborn crinkles behind knees and around the waistband are pressed smooth without flattening the garment's natural 3D shape.

    Targeted Treatment

    Tough Stains? We Treat Them Before Cleaning.

    Jeans face some of the most stubborn everyday marks. Stains require careful assessment and pre-treatment before entering the main cleaning cycle.

    Food & Beverage

    Gravy, coffee, tea, oil drips, sauces, and condiment marks

    Oil & Grease

    Automobile grease, bike chain oil, cosmetics, and cooking oils

    Sweat & Body Oils

    Waistband grime, inner-thigh friction marks, and odor zones

    Mud & Grass Marks

    Rain puddle splashes, lower hemline mud, and lawn stains

    Dust & Street Grime

    Daily commute soot, airborne dust, and surface dullness

    Ink & Everyday Marks

    Pen marks in pocket bags and belt leather dye rub-off

    Pre-Treatment Before Main Cycle

    Every garment is inspected individually upon collection. Targeted spotting agents are applied to loosen oils and grime before deep cleaning begins.

    Important Care Notice: Treatment results depend on the type of stain, fabric condition, age of the stain and previous treatment. While our specialists use professional spot-lifting techniques, complete removal cannot be guaranteed for every aged or heat-set mark.
