Scope of Services

Wash4You provides:

  • Laundry (wash & fold, wash & iron), dry cleaning, and pressing
  • Shoe and bag cleaning, as well as leather and suede care
  • Curtain and carpet cleaning services

All offerings include complimentary home pickup and delivery within designated service areas, as determined by management. Curtain cleaning includes free removal and reinstallation after service. Typical turnaround time is 5 to 7 working days from the pickup date. No compensation will be provided for delays arising from logistical, environmental, or technical challenges.

Claims & Complaints

All claims regarding lost, missing, or damaged items must be submitted within 24 hours of delivery and must include the original invoice. Claims submitted after this period will not be entertained.
